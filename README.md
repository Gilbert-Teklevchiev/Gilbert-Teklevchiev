## Research

### Assessing the effect of flash crashes on the U.S. Stock Market

My 2022 master's thesis at LUT University examines the market effects of the May 2010, August 2015, and February 2018 flash crashes. The study applies the Yang-Zhang range-based volatility estimator and statistical hypothesis testing to the VIX and U.S. large-, mid-, and small-cap indexes across 252 trading days per event year.

The analysis found significant volatility and market distress associated with flash crashes, with the large-cap index showing greater susceptibility than the mid- and small-cap indexes in the evaluated periods.
