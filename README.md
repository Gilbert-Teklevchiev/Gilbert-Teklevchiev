## Research

### Assessing the Effect of Flash Crashes on the U.S. Stock Market

My master's thesis at LUT University evaluates the May 2010, August 2015, and February 2018 flash crashes using the **Yang-Zhang range-based volatility estimator** and statistical hypothesis testing. The study analyzes the VIX and U.S. large-, mid-, and small-cap indexes across 252 trading days per event year.

The results identified significant volatility and market distress associated with flash crashes, with the large-cap index showing greater susceptibility than the mid- and small-cap indexes during the evaluated periods.
